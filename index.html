Part of Something Bigger

The International USE-IT Network

We aren't flying solo. USE-IT England is a proud member of the broader USE-IT Europe network. For over 20 years, this initiative has united local youth organisations across the continent to create honest, un-commercial city guides.

From Oslo to Prague, dozens of European cities have their own maps designed by local youth. Planning your next trip beyond England?
